Privacy Policy

Data Protection Governance

Overview and Scope of the Privacy Policy

Legal Rights Center is a law firm that provides legal representation and advice in areas such as civil rights, corporate law, criminal defense, and estate planning. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, protect, share, and store personal data we gather through our interactions with clients.

This policy applies to all data collection methods, both online and offline, that we manage. This includes our website at legalrightscenter.online, emails sent to legalrightscenter.online@gmail.com, phone calls to +1 874 555 8080, intake forms, and interactions on our social media profiles like Instagram (@legalrightscenter.online) and Reddit (u/legalrightscenter).

Important Notice on Attorney-Client Privilege: Sending information to Legal Rights Center through our website, email, phone, or social media does not create an attorney-client relationship. Such a relationship is only established when a signed engagement agreement is executed by an authorized attorney after a conflict-of-interest check. Any unsolicited information sent before this agreement is not protected by attorney-client privilege and may not be confidential.

Information We Collect

Legal Rights Center collects personal data through voluntary disclosures, automated technical processes, and third-party platforms to assess legal inquiries, fulfill contracts, and secure our operations.

Personal Information Provided Voluntarily

Individuals who interact with Legal Rights Center provide personal information needed for identity verification and initial legal assessments. This information includes names, addresses, phone numbers, and email addresses. Prospective clients may also submit additional records when inquiring about civil rights violations, corporate structuring, criminal defense, or estate planning, including employment histories, financial statements, corporate documents, tax records, legal backgrounds, or estate asset lists.

Automated Technical and Usage Data

When users visit legalrightscenter.online, our servers automatically collect technical data including IP addresses, web browser types, device specifications, operating system details, referral URLs, timestamps, pages viewed, and click patterns. We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to ensure our website works correctly, diagnose errors, measure traffic, and improve accessibility.

Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Cookie Policy

This website uses Google AdSense, a third-party advertising service provided by Google LLC. Google and third-party vendors use cookies, web beacons, and unique device identifiers to serve personalized advertisements to visitors based on their prior visits to legalrightscenter.online or other websites across the Internet.

  • Google Advertising Cookies: Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve targeted ads based on user interaction and web browsing history.
  • User Cookie Choice & Opt-Out: Visitors may opt out of personalized Google advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, users may opt out of third-party vendor advertising cookies by visiting aboutads.info.
  • Log Files & Web Beacons: Like most standard web servers, we log IP addresses, browser types, internet service providers (ISPs), referring/exit pages, and time stamps for site administration and security purposes.

Compliance Note: This policy complies strictly with Google AdSense Publisher Terms and GDPR/CCPA consumer privacy disclosure mandates.

How We Use Your Information

Legal Rights Center processes personal data based on specific legal grounds and operational needs. We use data solely for activities necessary to evaluate, deliver, and safeguard our legal services.

  • Evaluating Legal Inquiries: Reviewing case viability across civil rights, corporate law, criminal defense, and estate planning.
  • Conflict-of-Interest Audits: Internal verification to prevent representation conflicts under bar ethics rules.
  • Delivering Legal Representation: Filing court papers, drafting agreements, managing corporate entities, and handling trial advocacy.
  • Cybersecurity & System Protection: Enforcing TLS encryption and network threat monitoring.

Data Protection & Commercial Non-Sale Policy

Legal Rights Center has a strict policy against commercializing personal data. We never sell, rent, lease, trade, or monetize personal information, contact details, consultation inquiries, or web analytics to data brokers or advertisers.

Your Statutory Privacy Rights

Under applicable privacy statutes, individuals interacting with Legal Rights Center possess statutory rights including the Right to Access, Right to Rectification, Right to Erasure (Deletion), Right to Restrict or Object, Right to Withdraw Consent, and Right to Non-Discrimination.

To exercise any privacy rights, submit a formal written request to our privacy team at legalrightscenter.online@gmail.com with the subject line "Privacy Rights Request." Verified requests are processed within thirty (30) days of receipt.
